Bayan

Fertility · Challenge 03 · Understanding and Reducing Inherited Disease Risk

Abu Dhabi's premarital genetic results take up to 14 days. Today that wait is empty. Bayan uses it to prepare the couple and the family elders who shape the decision, by voice, so that everyone arrives at the counsellor's desk already understanding what a result can and cannot mean.

Day 0 · Enrolment at the blood draw

Bayan is a layer inside the existing premarital pathway, not another app to download. Each partner scans a code at the clinic and enrols privately on their own phone. The household is the unit: each partner may invite one family decision-maker.

Family elder

Umm Khalid, 63

  • Joins by answering a phone call or a voice message. No reading, no typing, no account.
  • Hears the same lessons as the couple, in Gulf Arabic.
  • Never receives anyone's result. The education track carries general knowledge only.
  • Can leave at any time by saying so.

Why elders are in the core design: marriages here are family decisions, and a result that only the couple understands is easily overruled or misread at home. Every consent above can be withdrawn and is written to an access log.

Days 1–13 · Three people, one curriculum

How every answer is built, and why it can be audited

The model never writes freely and never calculates. It can only select and simplify claims from a clinician-approved library. A checker rejects any sentence that does not trace to a claim. Anything else is handed to a human. Pick a question:

Day 14 · The result comes from a person

Bayan never delivers a result. The physician privileged in genetic counselling does, as the Department of Health standard requires. Bayan supplies the picture, the numbers computed by code, and the privacy controls.

Synthetic result

Both partners carry a variant in the same gene

For each pregnancy, out of 4 possible outcomes:

has the condition
healthy carrier
healthy carrier
not a carrier

1 in 4 chance of a child with the condition, 2 in 4 a healthy carrier, 1 in 4 neither. The chance is the same for every pregnancy. C-05

This is a chance for future children, not a diagnosis of either of you. The counsellor will explain the options available to you. The decision is yours.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a scripted mock with no live model and no real data. The claim library shown is a sample and has not been clinically reviewed. How Abu Dhabi delivers results digitally is not confirmed, so the integration point is an assumption. Faith-based content is intentionally out of scope.
